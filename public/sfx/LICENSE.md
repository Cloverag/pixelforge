(https://kenney.nl). Each pack's own `License.txt` says *Creative Commons Zero, CC0*
(http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), checked when the files were chosen:

- Sci-Fi Sounds 1.0 - https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
